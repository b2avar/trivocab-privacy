This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use the Trivocab mobile app (the “App”).

In short: You don’t need an account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location. Your learning progress stays encrypted on your device. The App shows no ads and we never sell your data. To fix bugs and improve the App we use Unity’s analytics and crash reporting service only if you allow it. We ask on first launch, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Share usage data. If you buy Trivocab Plus, the payment is taken by Apple or Google; we never see your card details. To verify your purchase, an anonymous ID and the purchase record are shared with our service provider RevenueCat.

Burak Avar (developer of the App)

This data is not linked to your name, email or any other identity, is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking, and is never sold.

Features that stay on your device

  • Pronunciation: English words are read aloud by your device’s own speech engine, offline.
  • Daily reminder: Notifications are scheduled on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off in your device settings or with the Daily reminder switch in the App.
  • Android backup: On Android, the progress file may be part of your phone’s automatic backup to your Google account. Google manages this backup; we cannot access it.
  • Support email: Settings › Contact us opens your email app with the app version, device model, OS and language added below your message. It reaches us only if you send it; your email address and these details are used only to reply to you.

Paid subscription (Trivocab Plus)

  • Levels A1 and A2, and the first 10 chapters of B1, B2 and C1, are free. For the rest you can buy Trivocab Plus monthly, yearly (with a 7-day free trial) or as a one-time lifetime purchase.
  • Payment is taken by Apple or Google through your App Store or Google Play account. Your card and payment details never reach us.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. You can cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings; if you cancel before a free trial ends, you are not charged.
  • Refund requests are handled by Apple or Google under their own rules.
  • You can unlock Plus bought on another device with Settings › Restore purchases, using the same store account.

3. Sharing and international transfers

Usage and technical data are shared with our service provider Unity Technologies (based in the USA; Unity Gaming Services – Analytics and Diagnostics) and processed on Unity’s servers outside Türkiye, mainly in the USA, which may be outside your country. Unity’s terms: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy · Unity Data Processing Addendum. Purchase and subscription data is shared with our service provider RevenueCat, Inc. to verify purchases and processed on its servers in the USA: RevenueCat Privacy Policy · RevenueCat Data Processing Addendum. Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We may disclose data to public authorities where required by law. We do not share it with anyone else.

4. How long we keep it

  • Progress on your device: until you delete it (Settings › Reset progress, or uninstalling the App).
  • Usage and technical data: up to 13 months at Unity, after which it is deleted or anonymised.
  • Purchase and subscription records: while your Plus access continues and for the retention periods required by tax and commercial law; then deleted or anonymised.

5. Security

The progress file is encrypted with AES-256 and signed. Its key is not part of the App: it is generated for each installation and stored in the iOS Keychain or in Android’s app-private storage. Communication with Unity and RevenueCat uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

6. Consent, turning off and deleting usage data

When the App is first opened it asks for your permission for usage data and crash reports. If you decline, none of this data is sent and every feature of the App still works. When you switch off Settings › Share usage data, new data stops being sent and a request to delete the usage data already sent to Unity is submitted automatically. You can also email us to request deletion.

Purchase records are needed for Plus to work, so this switch does not affect them. You can ask us by email to delete them from RevenueCat; if you have an active subscription, cancel it in your store first. Payment records kept by Apple and Google are their responsibility.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Türkiye’s KVKK and the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Contact us at b2avargames@gmail.com; we will reply within 30 days, free of charge.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page, and significant changes will also be announced in the App.
