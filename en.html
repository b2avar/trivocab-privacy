This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use the Trivocab mobile app (the “App”).

In short: You don’t need an account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location. Your learning progress stays encrypted on your device. The App shows no ads and we never sell your data. We use Unity’s analytics and crash reporting service to fix bugs and improve the App; you can turn this off any time in Settings › Share usage data.

1. Who is responsible

Burak Avar (developer of the App)
Contact: b2avargames@gmail.com

2. What we process, why, and on what basis

DataWhere it is storedPurposeLegal basis
Learning progress (completed lessons, stars, badges, streak, saved and weak words) and app settings Only on your device, in an encrypted and signed file. Never sent to us. Running the App and letting you continue where you left off Necessary to provide the service
Encrypted copy of your progress (iPhone/iPad only) Your own iCloud account (Apple). We cannot access it. Syncing progress between your devices signed in with the same Apple ID Necessary to provide the service
Usage data: completed lessons and reviews, score, number of mistakes, badges claimed, level test result, session start/end Unity Technologies servers Understanding how the App is used and improving lessons and difficulty Legitimate interest; can be turned off in Settings
Technical data and crash reports: IP address, a random installation ID, the iOS identifier for vendor (IDFV), device model, OS and language, app version, crash and error logs Unity Technologies servers Detecting and fixing crashes and errors, keeping the service secure Legitimate interest

This data is not linked to your name, email or any other identity, is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking, and is never sold.

Features that stay on your device

  • Pronunciation: English words are read aloud by your device’s own speech engine, offline.
  • Daily reminder: Notifications are scheduled on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off in your device settings or with the Daily reminder switch in the App.
  • Android backup: On Android, the progress file may be part of your phone’s automatic backup to your Google account. Google manages this backup; we cannot access it.

3. Sharing and international transfers

Usage and technical data are shared with our service provider Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services – Analytics and Diagnostics), whose servers are located outside Türkiye and may be outside your country. Unity’s terms: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. We may disclose data to public authorities where required by law. We do not share it with anyone else.

4. How long we keep it

  • Progress on your device: until you delete it (Settings › Reset progress, or uninstalling the App).
  • Usage and technical data: up to 13 months at Unity, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

5. Security

The progress file is encrypted with AES-256 and signed. Its key is not part of the App: it is generated for each installation and stored in the iOS Keychain or in Android’s app-private storage. Communication with Unity uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

6. Turning off and deleting usage data

When you switch off Settings › Share usage data, new data stops being sent and a request to delete the usage data already sent to Unity is submitted automatically. You can also email us to request deletion.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Türkiye’s KVKK and the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Contact us at b2avargames@gmail.com; we will reply within 30 days, free of charge.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page, and significant changes will also be announced in the App.
